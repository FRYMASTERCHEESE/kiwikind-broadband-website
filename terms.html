Broadband orders and account terms

Updated 22 October 2026. These terms explain the KiwiKind website, free account and order review process. They are not your final broadband service contract. The service-specific consumer agreement, approved prices, installation conditions and retail terms must be separately reviewed and accepted before payment or service activation.

Who operates the site?

KiwiKind Broadband Limited, NZBN 9429054036550, New Zealand company number 9466791. Contact kiwikindbroadband@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or corrections. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The account portal uses a separate secure backend shared by the Android app.

Choose a plan and request a connection

Browse the broadband catalogue, select your speed, data and installation or router options, and submit your service address and contact details. All displayed prices are NZD including GST. The totals include exactly $3 extra monthly and $10 extra setup once; GST is not added again to these additions.

Submitting an order requests an address and connection check. No payment is taken and no service is activated at that stage. Prices labelled “to confirm”, usage charges and eligibility restrictions must be resolved in your final offer. Secondary fibre options require an eligible existing connection. Speeds and availability depend on your address and network.

Confirmation before payment

KiwiKind reviews the service address and connection details manually. Once confirmed, your final offer appears under Billing in My KiwiKind on the website and app. It must state the selected service, full monthly and setup amounts, payment start, installation, equipment, contract, cancellation and refund terms. You can then accept that offer and continue to Stripe.

Creating an account or submitting an order does not accept a minimum term or authorise cancellation of your existing connection. Do not cancel your current broadband until switching and installation arrangements are confirmed.

Website accounts and security

Use accurate account contact information and keep credentials secure. Google sign-in and email/password sign-in use the separate shared backend. Do not send passwords, reset links, card details or identity documents in support messages. Data and deletion choices are covered by our privacy policy.

Final offers and consumer rights

Every final offer needs separate confirmation of service address, supplier availability, approved GST-inclusive monthly and one-off prices, speed limitations, equipment, minimum term, cancellation, switching authority, installation and support arrangements. Nothing here excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under New Zealand law, including applicable rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and Fair Trading Act 1986.

Support, complaints and account deletion

Contact kiwikindbroadband@gmail.com for account support or complaints. No live service-support SLA or membership in a telecommunications dispute scheme is promised unless stated in your confirmed service terms. Account deletion instructions are available without signing in.

Changes

We may update these order and account terms. Changes do not alter an offer you have already accepted. This page alone does not authorise charges or supplier provisioning; payment requires your separate acceptance of the final offer.
