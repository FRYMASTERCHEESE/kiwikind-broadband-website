Pre-launch website and account terms

Updated 21 October 2026. This is pre-launch information for the KiwiKind Broadband website and free customer account, not a residential broadband service contract. The service-specific consumer agreement, approved prices, installation conditions and retail terms must be separately reviewed and accepted before any service can be ordered.

Who operates the site?

KiwiKind Broadband Limited, NZBN 9429054036550, New Zealand company number 9466791. Contact kiwikindbroadband@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or corrections. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The account portal uses a separate secure backend shared by the Android app.

What you can do during pre-launch

Read information about the planned service, create a free KiwiKind account, access your own account data and use available support/privacy channels. Information about planned speeds and services is exploratory; do not rely on it as an offer, coverage guarantee or installation confirmation.

What you cannot buy yet

No live broadband products, confirmed general retail prices, installation appointments, supplier orders or customer payment collection are available. Creating an account does not order a broadband service, accept a minimum term, or authorise cancellation of your existing connection. Do not cancel your current broadband because you registered here.

Website accounts and security

Use accurate account contact information and keep credentials secure. Google sign-in and email/password sign-in use the separate shared backend. Do not send passwords, reset links, card details or identity documents in support messages. Data and deletion choices are covered by our privacy policy.

Future offers and consumer rights

Any future offer needs separate confirmation of service address, supplier availability, approved GST-inclusive monthly and one-off prices, speed limitations, equipment, minimum term, cancellation, switching authority, installation and support arrangements. Nothing here excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under New Zealand law, including applicable rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and Fair Trading Act 1986.

Support, complaints and account deletion

Contact kiwikindbroadband@gmail.com for account support or complaints. No live service-support SLA or membership in a telecommunications dispute scheme is promised at this pre-launch stage. Account deletion instructions are available without signing in.

Changes

We may update these pre-launch terms. Before live sales, customer terms and applicable consumer, support, tax and privacy obligations require independent review. This page does not authorise live payments or supplier provisioning.

Private payment offers

Creating an account does not authorise charges. Any private payment offer must identify the specific service, full GST-inclusive monthly and one-time setup amounts, payment start, installation, equipment, contract, cancellation and refund terms before acceptance. A customer must separately accept that offer before Stripe checkout. Payment does not automatically activate broadband or place a supplier order. Contact KiwiKind about refunds and broadband cancellation; stopping subscription payments alone does not cancel contractual service obligations.
