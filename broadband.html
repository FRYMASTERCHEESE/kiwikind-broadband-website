Broadband types, made simple

If you have a fibre box (ONT) on the wall, start here. Usually best for home streaming, gaming and working from home if we can supply the address.

☎️ Copper (ADSL/VDSL)

Can I use my existing Spark, One NZ or other modem/router?

Sometimes, but not every modem is compatible. Fibre uses a fibre box (ONT) and a router with an Ethernet WAN/Internet port. A DSL-only phone-line modem cannot connect directly to fibre. Some provider routers are locked or cannot use the network settings KiwiKind needs.

For 500 Mbps fibre, a router with gigabit WAN/LAN ports is recommended. Faster Hyperfibre services need higher-speed hardware. RBI and fixed wireless may need a SIM-compatible router or other approved equipment.

We need to check your exact router model, connection type and supplier settings first. Ask KiwiKind to check your router model →

What do the fibre speeds mean?

100/20 Mbps is generally enough for lighter browsing and streaming. 500/100 Mbps is a useful starting point for busy households with several people, video calls, gaming or streaming. 950/500 Mbps and Hyperfibre are for heavier use and need suitable equipment. Actual speeds and availability vary.
