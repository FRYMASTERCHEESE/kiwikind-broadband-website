Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign up for broadband today?

Not yet. KiwiKind is a pre-launch project, not an operating internet service provider.

Will KiwiKind offer cheaper fibre?

Affordable fibre is our goal. We cannot promise prices until wholesale costs and business requirements are confirmed.

Is there an Android app?

An Android app is in development. It isn't available for public customer use yet.

Can I check availability or pay?

No. Coverage checks, orders and payments are disabled until the service is ready.
